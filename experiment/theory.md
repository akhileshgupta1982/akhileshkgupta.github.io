<p>For positive half cycle, thyristors T<sub>1</sub> and T<sub>4</sub> both are gated at an angle α and both thyristors conduct from ωt=α to ωt=π+α. Therefore, the current conduction starts in the direction V<sub>s</sub> →T<sub>1</sub>→R→T<sub>4</sub>→V<sub>s</sub> as shown by red line for π radian. Therefore, the output voltage is given by:</p>
V<sub>o</sub> = V<sub>s</sub><br>

<p align="justify"> At to ωt=π+α, thyristors T<sub>3</sub> and T<sub>2</sub> both which are already forward biased, are triggered together and current conduction starts in the direction V<sub>s</sub>→T<sub>3</sub>→R→T<sub>2</sub>→V<sub>s</sub>. The input supply voltage turns off T<sub>1</sub>and T<sub>4</sub> by natural commutation. Therefore, the output voltage is given by:</p>
V<sub>o</sub> =-V<sub>s</sub><br>
