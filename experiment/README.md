### Aim
To study single phase fully controlled bridge rectifiers with resistive and inductive loads.
### Theory
Most commonly used controlled rectifier for the speed control of DC machine is single phase fully controlled rectifier. The circuit diagram of fully controlled rectifier with R load and corresponding output waveform is shown in figure 1. To understand the working of this circuit, click on next button. The average DC load voltage in fully controlled rectifier is controlled by the controlling the firing angle (α) for the two transistors in per half cycle.
### Procedure
1. Read the instruction to aware with use of simulator.
2. Go to theory section and after reading theory click on next button to understand the operating principle with R load by visual effect.
3. After completion of theory, go to calculation section and provide inputs.
4. Click on Average Voltage button.
5. Analyse the effect of firing angle on output.
6. Click on RL load button at the top right corner to toggle the window to RL load page.
7. Repeat the process 2 to 9 to study operating principle with RL load.
8. Click on Print button to save the output.
